Gustavus Jazz Lab Band

The performances by the jazz band were a small-town treat. Often the band had students that I knew from my classes at the college. The director was a trumpet player and often produced talented trumpet sections. Also shown here is a guest trombonist. The college president's son (a physics major in our department) is also seen soloing on his saxophone.
